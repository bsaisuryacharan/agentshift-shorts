## 3. What the channel's own data says about topics (n=26-30, small: a hypothesis, shrunk and capped in code)
Winners: awe/scale with a familiar noun ("Space Is Too Big For Your Brain" 1.7k), "inside a machine" ("Inside a Submarine" 1.1k), myth-vs-fact on something known (ozone hole 980), what-ifs with a planet or volcano (Jupiter 873).
Flops: jargon (Cosmic Microwave Background 55, Solar Sails 39), abstract history/institutions (Y2K 37, Svalbard 102), AI-tips listicles (24-33).
Learned class boosts on the live data: what_if +0.31, myth_fact +0.29, inside_machine +0.23, ai_tips -0.47, jargon_science -0.27 (the picker adds them to a curiosity rubric).

## 4. What is still open
1. Real render engines per style (Lemo-Opuscar-class): a parametrised scene kit per look, rendered in CI. Plan in `docs/FACELESS_TOOLKIT.md`.
2. Per-video retention and swipe-away: needs the YouTube Analytics token per video, not the channel aggregate.
3. Set the repo variable `OPENROUTER_VISION_MODEL` (a vision-capable model) so the judge survives a Gemini 429.
4. Brand decision: science explainers (what the data supports) or AI-agents/business (what the bio says).
5. After the first week of results: re-fit `topicscore` classes and `stylemap` look results (both read `data/features.jsonl` + `assets/performance.json`).
